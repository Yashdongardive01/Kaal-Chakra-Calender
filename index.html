Kaal Chakra: Research Paper & Evaluation Guide

Research Title

Kaal Chakra: Algorithmic Modeling and Sociocultural Synchronization of the Vedic Lunisolar Calendar System

Abstract (~250 Words)

The traditional Indian calendar (Panchang) represents one of the world's most sophisticated lunisolar astronomical systems, harmonizing lunar phases (Tithis), solar ingress (Sankrantis), and terrestrial seasonal cycles (Ritu Chakra). This research analyzes the mathematical, astronomical, and cultural framework of the Vedic calendar to examine how traditional timekeeping governs sociocultural life, agricultural cycles, and seasonal health across India. Using a computational modeling methodology, we developed the interactive Kaal Chakra platform to simulate the lunisolar reconciliation mechanism—specifically the intercalary Adhik Maas correction—and compare the Purnimanta and Amanta systems alongside regional variations. Furthermore, the study correlates traditional seasonal practices (Ritucharya) with astronomical solstices and equinoxes, demonstrating the scientific basis behind festival scheduling and fasting observances such as Ekadashi and Chaturmas. The significance of this study lies in bridging ancient Indian astronomy with modern web engineering, demonstrating how traditional calendars preserve environmental and physiological synchrony. The unique contribution of our project is an open-access, responsive digital interface that translates multi-layered astronomical algorithms into an intuitive, community-oriented tool for Indian households and academic scholars.
